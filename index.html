Quick start

Library

Pick a video file (mp4, mov, webm…) and Pulse keeps just the soundtrack, re-compressed small. Nothing is uploaded — the conversion happens on your phone.

From a link: paste a direct media link and Pulse fetches it — audio links stream and can be saved offline, video links are downloaded and reduced to audio automatically. From another app: once installed, Pulse appears in Android's share sheet, so you can share a downloaded file or a media link into it directly.
