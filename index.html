Library

Pick a video file (mp4, mov, webm…) and Pulse keeps just the soundtrack, re-compressed small. Nothing is uploaded — the conversion happens on your phone.

From TikTok, X or Instagram: use that app's own Save video button to put the file on your phone, then share it into Pulse from the Android share sheet — Pulse keeps the soundtrack and drops the picture. Paste one of their links here and Pulse tells you exactly which button to press.

From a YouTube link: paste one and Pulse offers the routes that actually work — Studio download for your own uploads, background listening in Firefox, or finding an audio version.

From a link: paste a direct media link and Pulse fetches it — audio links stream and can be saved offline, video links are downloaded and reduced to audio automatically. From another app: once installed, Pulse appears in Android's share sheet, so you can share a downloaded file or a media link into it directly.

Names & tidying

Rename one track from its … menu in the library. This does several at once — useful when a batch of files came in with rubbish names.

Measure a folder

Point this at a music folder and it reports what is in there and what the same music would weigh re-encoded — without importing or changing anything. It only reads names and sizes, so even a huge drive takes seconds. Works best on a computer: open this same page in Chrome there and pick the drive.
